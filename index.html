Piezopeening Coverage Simulator

A piezoelectric actuator drives a spherical tool up and down at frequency f while the CNC machine moves it along straight lines at the feed velocity Vfeed. The tool tip penetration follows h(t) = Uoff + a·sin(2πf·t); each time h > 0 the sphere indents the surface. The page traces every dent, counts how many times each point is hit and measures the coverage.

Local coverage of a point = 100 % × number of times it has been hit. The four shares add up to 100 % of the measured zone; the global coverage is everything except the 0 % share.

Piezopeening places impacts on a regular lattice, so the number of hits per point is far more uniform than with random shot peening. A random process with the same mean would leave the share shown by the purple dots untouched.

Each point keeps the deepest trace of the sphere over all impacts: depth = max over impacts of [h − (R − √(R² − d²))], d = distance to the tool axis. Theoretical ridge height between neighbouring traces at spacing e: R − √(R² − (e/2)²), with e = p across the feed and e = s along it (tool travel during contact neglected). Kinematic model: no elastic recovery or pile-up.

Click Run all to compare both processes at the same stage. Random impacts with mean λ leave about e−λ of the surface untouched and spread the hits widely (standard deviation ≈ √λ). Below the gap-free step-over, the piezopeening lattice covers everything with a much narrower spread of hits. Above it, the lattice leaves untreated strips between the lines that random impacts would have covered. The random column changes slightly from one draw to the next because the zone holds only a few hundred impacts.

Parameters

Sign convention: h > 0 means the tool tip is below the initial surface, in the material. Uoff > 0 moves the median line of the oscillation into the material (hmax = Uoff + a). If your offset is measured away from the part, enter a negative value. Changing a parameter restarts the simulation.

  1. Spacing of impacts along a lines = Vfeed / f
  2. Dent radius at maximum penetrationhmax = η·(Uoff + a); r = √(2R·hmax − hmax²), R = tool radius
  3. Footprint of one impactunion of the contact circles while the tool moves V·tc during the contact time tc
  4. Impact velocity and anglefirst contact when Uoff + a·sin θ = 0: vn = 2πf·√(a² − Uoff²); vt = Vfeed; |v| = √(vn² + vt²); angle from normal = atan(vt / vn)
  5. Dent aspect ratioW = 2r; L = 2·max over the contact of (V·|t − tpeak| + r(t)); ≈ round while V·tc ≪ 2r
  6. Mean hits per point per passλ = A / (s · p), p = step-over between lines
  7. Gap-free condition (circular dents)(p/2)² + (s/2)² ≤ r² (aligned lines); staggered lines tolerate a larger p
  8. Treatment ratearea per second = Vfeed · p; time for 1 cm² = 100 / (Vfeed·p)
